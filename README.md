## 🎯 Project Overview

Organizations often struggle to understand why employees leave, which departments are most affected, and how workforce trends impact business performance.

This HR Analytics Dashboard was developed in Power BI to uncover hidden attrition patterns, monitor workforce metrics, and support strategic HR decision-making through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

---

## 🚨 Business Problem

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations as it leads to:

- Increased recruitment and training costs
- Loss of experienced employees
- Reduced team productivity
- Delays in business operations
- Lower employee morale

The HR department needed a centralized dashboard to identify the key drivers of attrition and take proactive measures to improve employee retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

✔ Analyze employee attrition trends

✔ Identify high-risk employee groups

✔ Understand the impact of salary, age, education, and job roles on attrition

✔ Support workforce planning and retention strategies

✔ Enable data-driven HR decision-making

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 Employee Age Analysis
- Employees aged **26–35 years** recorded the highest attrition.
- Early-career professionals are more likely to seek better opportunities and career growth.

### 💰 Salary Impact on Attrition
- Employees earning **Up to 5K** account for the majority of attrition cases.
- Compensation appears to be a significant factor influencing employee turnover.

### 🎓 Education Analysis
- Employees from **Life Sciences** and **Medical** backgrounds contributed the highest attrition levels.
- Specialized talent retention strategies may be required.

### 💼 Job Role Analysis
Highest attrition was observed among:

- Laboratory Technician
- Sales Executive
- Research Scientist
- Sales Representative

These roles should be prioritized for retention initiatives.

### 👨‍💼 Gender Analysis
- Male employees experienced higher attrition compared to female employees.

### ⏳ Tenure Analysis
- Most employees left during their early years with the company.
- Indicates opportunities to improve onboarding and employee engagement programs.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

### 1️⃣ Compensation Optimization
- Review salary structures for lower salary bands.
- Introduce performance-based rewards and incentives.

### 2️⃣ Employee Retention Programs
- Develop targeted retention plans for employees aged 26–35.
- Strengthen career growth opportunities.

### 3️⃣ High-Risk Role Management
- Conduct periodic satisfaction surveys for high-attrition roles.
- Improve workload distribution and employee support.

### 4️⃣ Early Employee Engagement
- Enhance onboarding experiences.
- Implement mentorship and professional development programs.
